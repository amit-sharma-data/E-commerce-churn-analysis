# E-Commerce Customer Churn & Revenue Analysis

## 📌 Project Overview

This project analyzes e-commerce customer behavior to identify customer churn patterns, customer segments, and revenue opportunities.

The analysis uses **RFM (Recency, Frequency, Monetary) segmentation** and cohort analysis to understand customer retention and identify high-value customers.

## 🎯 Objectives

- Analyze customer purchasing behavior
- Identify customers at risk of churn
- Segment customers using RFM analysis
- Analyze customer retention using cohort analysis
- Identify revenue and retention opportunities
- Generate actionable business insights

## 🛠️ Tools & Technologies

- Python
- Pandas
- NumPy
- Matplotlib
- Seaborn
- Jupyter Notebook
- RFM Analysis
- Cohort Analysis

## 📊 Analysis Performed

### 1. Data Cleaning
- Handled missing values
- Removed duplicate records
- Prepared transaction data for analysis

### 2. RFM Segmentation
Customers were analyzed based on:

- **Recency** – How recently a customer purchased
- **Frequency** – How often a customer purchased
- **Monetary** – How much a customer spent

### 3. Customer Churn Analysis
Identified customer groups with lower engagement and potential churn risk.

### 4. Cohort Analysis
Analyzed customer retention over time to understand how customer engagement changes after their first purchase.

## 💡 Key Business Insight

The analysis identifies a significant customer retention opportunity of approximately **$454K**, highlighting the potential revenue impact of improving customer retention and re-engaging at-risk customers.
